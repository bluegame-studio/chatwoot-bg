# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customer support agents and team leads working through active conversations. They need to understand customer messages and prepare accurate replies quickly, often across languages and channels.

## Product Purpose

Chatwoot is a customer support workspace for managing conversations across channels. Success means agents can move from context to a correct, sendable response with minimal interruption and without losing draft content or conversation state.

## Brand Personality

Clear, capable, and approachable. The interface should feel dependable during repeated operational work while keeping the conversation itself at the center.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard patterns, hidden primary workflow controls, modal-heavy interactions, and unfamiliar controls that slow down routine conversation handling.

## Design Principles

- Keep the active conversation and reply workflow visible.
- Prefer familiar, compact controls for repeated agent actions.
- Preserve user state across repeated work where it reduces reconfiguration.
- Reveal feedback and recoverable actions close to the content they affect.
- Match existing Chatwoot component and interaction conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic labels, readable contrast, and layouts that remain usable without relying on color alone.
